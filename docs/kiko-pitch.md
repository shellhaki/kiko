# Kiko — The Pitch

Nov 8, 2026 · @Excel

## Every idea deserves a plan

**Kiko turns a one-line idea into a real document in minutes: a proposal, a spec, a pitch deck or a budget sheet. It works with whatever AI you can afford, and it's free and open source.**

You talk. Kiko writes. You watch the document build itself beside the chat, section by section, and when it's done it's yours to edit, export and share.

## The problem

We all have ideas. Most of them die in the notes app.

Turning an idea into something real means writing it down properly: what it is, who it's for, how it works, what it costs. That's hours of work most people never get to. The AI tools that do it well cost a monthly subscription in dollars, which many students and young builders simply can't keep paying.

The free alternatives aren't much better. Ask a regular chatbot for a project plan and you get a wall of text in a chat bubble. You can't edit it properly, you lose it in the scroll, and you have to copy it out and reformat it before anyone else can read it.

**Good planning is locked behind a paywall, and the free tools give you chat messages, not documents.**

## Meet Kiko

Picture this. It's 2 a.m. and you've just had an idea for a creator platform. You open Kiko and type:

> *"I want to build a platform where fans pay for specific pieces of work, not just tip creators. Write me a proposal."*

Here's what happens next:

1. **Kiko researches.** You see it searching the web live: competitors, payment options, what's missing in the market.
2. **The document appears beside the chat.** First the outline, then each section fills in while you watch: the problem, the product, why it wins, the risks.
3. **Kiko asks where it's unsure.** Small questions appear right inside the document ("Start with developers or musicians?"), with buttons to answer in one tap.
4. **You shape it.** Type straight into the document, or tell Kiko "make the business model section punchier". It changes only what you asked for.
5. **You share it.** One click exports a clean PDF or Word file, ready to send to your team or a hackathon judge.

From idea to shareable proposal in one conversation, the way the best paid AI tools do it, but on any model you choose.

## What Kiko can do

- **Write real documents.** Proposals, specs, pitch docs, reports and plans, each as a live, editable document, not a chat message.
- **Build spreadsheets and decks.** Budgets and trackers you can calculate with, and slide decks ready to present.
- **Export to anything.** PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Markdown, in one click.
- **Research the web.** Connect a web search key and Kiko looks up current facts before writing them, with sources linked in the document.
- **Ask smart questions.** When it's unsure, it leaves a question right on the paragraph, with tap-to-answer buttons.
- **Show its work.** Every step it takes (searching, writing, editing) appears in the chat as it happens, so you're never guessing what it did.
- **Still just chat.** Ask it anything and it answers normally. It only reaches for documents when you need one.

## Why it's different

**Bring your own AI.** Kiko doesn't sell you a subscription. You connect the AI model you want and pay only for what you use, often a few cents per document. It starts with Meta's Muse, which gives new accounts free credits, and more models are coming, including free and local ones.

**Documents first, not chat first.** Most AI apps are chat apps that happen to write long messages. Kiko is built around the document: it plans the outline, fills it in section by section, and edits only what you ask for, so your own changes are never overwritten.

**Free and open source.** Anyone can download it, read the code, check that their keys stay on their own computer, and help make it better.

**Your stuff stays yours.** Your documents and keys live on your machine. No account and no tracking.

## Who it's for

- **Students** writing project proposals, reports and final-year documents.
- **Hackathon teams** who need a pitch, a spec and a deck by morning.
- **Indie developers and founders** with more ideas than time to plan them.
- **Small studios and freelancers** writing proposals and specs for clients.

Anyone who plans with AI but doesn't want another monthly bill.

## What we're building first

Kiko starts as a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux. The first version does the core thing really well: chat with Muse, get a live document, edit it together, and export it. Then come web search, PDF and Word export, spreadsheets, slides, more AI models and a web version.

The first real test: **Kiko's own next proposal will be written in Kiko.**
